Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This notice explains how personal data are processed through the Playa Luna website, the connected venues and the Fior d'Acqua VIP Club, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”).

1. Data Controller

LIDO PLAYALUNA SRL, Via Marina di Varcaturo 42, 81030 Castel Volturno (CE), Italy — VAT no. 09146931218. Privacy requests can be sent to info@playaluna.it.

2. Data processed

  • identification and contact data, including name, telephone number and email address;
  • event, restaurant and beach booking information;
  • VIP card code, membership level, benefits, referrals and access status;
  • profile photograph, booking history, operational notes and warnings managed by authorised staff;
  • authentication, security and access-attempt logs;
  • technical navigation data, IP address, device and browser information.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • responding to requests and managing bookings: pre-contractual measures and performance of a contract;
  • managing the VIP Club, access control, benefits and check-in: performance of the requested service and legitimate interest in venue security;
  • fraud prevention, system security and misuse management: legitimate interest and compliance with legal obligations;
  • administrative and accounting obligations: compliance with legal obligations;
  • promotional communications and optional uses of photographs: consent, where required.

4. Processing methods and access

Data are processed electronically using access controls, staff roles and security measures proportionate to the service. VIP photographs are stored in a private area and are made available through time-limited links. Only authorised staff and appointed service providers may access personal data.

5. Service providers and international transfers

Technical providers may include Supabase for database, authentication and storage, Vercel for hosting, Resend for transactional email, and third-party services opened by the user such as WhatsApp/Meta, Google Maps, the beach-booking service and the QR-code provider. These providers act under their own terms or as processors, as applicable. Where data are transferred outside the European Economic Area, the safeguards required by the GDPR apply.

6. Retention

  • customer sessions expire after 24 hours;
  • failed-login records are normally removed after 30 days;
  • contact and booking requests are retained for the time needed to manage the request and ordinarily no longer than 24 months, unless legal obligations require otherwise;
  • VIP profile data and photographs are retained while membership is active and for the period necessary to manage disputes, security or legal obligations;
  • administrative and accounting data are retained for the period required by law.

7. Mandatory and optional data

Data marked as required are necessary to provide the requested service. Failure to provide them may prevent a booking, VIP access or a response. Optional data and marketing consent may be withheld or withdrawn without affecting other services.

8. Data subject rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing, and may withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority. Requests may be sent to info@playaluna.it.

9. Cookies and external content

The website uses technical functions and may display content or links provided by third parties. External services may process technical data under their own privacy and cookie policies. Browser settings can be used to limit non-essential cookies; where legally required, optional technologies must be activated only after consent.

10. Updates

This notice may be updated when services, providers or legal requirements change. The latest version is published on this page.
